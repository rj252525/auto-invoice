Questions

Frequently asked

Is my data really private?

Yes — architecturally, not just as a promise. The app is a local program: invoices, client details, and settings are stored on your computer only, and the AI models run on your own processor. The only network calls the app ever makes are downloading the optional AI model, validating a license key when you activate one, and checking for updates.

Does the AI need an internet connection or a subscription to an AI provider?

No. The assistant uses open-weights models (like Qwen and IBM Granite) running locally through llama.cpp. You download a model once inside the app — after that, AI features work with the network unplugged, and there's no per-use cost. AI can make mistakes, so the app always asks you to review generated invoices before sending them.

What does it run on?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) today. A macOS version (Apple Silicon) is finished and being prepared for release. AI features are optional and run best with 8 GB of RAM or more; invoicing itself runs on nearly anything.

What's in the free trial?

Everything. Every template, unlimited invoices, PDF export, and the full AI assistant for 14 days — no account or card required. After the trial, exports carry a watermark and AI pauses until a license is activated.

How does licensing work?

After purchase you receive a license key by email. Paste it into Settings → License and you're activated. You can deactivate a device anytime to move your license to a new machine. No always-online requirement — the app checks the license periodically and tolerates being offline for weeks.

Is the installer safe?

The installer and every executable inside it are code-signed by a Microsoft-verified publisher through the Trusted Signing program, and the SHA-256 checksum is published below. Windows SmartScreen may show a notice for the first days of a new release while download reputation builds — the publisher name confirms it's genuine.
