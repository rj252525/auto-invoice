Refund Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Simple version: if Auto Invoice Generator isn't for you, you get your money back.

14-day money-back guarantee

Every purchase — monthly, yearly, or lifetime — can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. Since the free trial already gives you the complete product for 14 days, you'll rarely need this — but it's here.

How to request one

Email info@autoinvoicegen.com from the address you purchased with (or reply to your receipt email) and say you'd like a refund. Refunds are issued through our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, back to your original payment method — typically within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

After a refund

Your license key is deactivated. The app returns to trial/unlicensed behavior, and you can keep using it that way or uninstall it.

Subscription renewals

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically; a reminder is sent before renewal, and you can cancel anytime from the link in your receipt. An accidental renewal is refundable within 14 days of the charge.
